Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected on this site and how it is used. Last updated: 2026.

What we collect

When you submit the Puppy Inquiry form, we collect the name, contact details (email or phone) and any message you choose to share. We do not collect this information anywhere else on the site, and no automatic tracking of visitors takes place.

How it's used

Information submitted through the inquiry form is used solely to respond to your enquiry about a puppy. It is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data storage & requests

Your details are kept only as long as needed to handle your enquiry. You can request that your information be corrected or deleted at any time by contacting us directly.
