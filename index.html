GOODTO KNOW

Still unsure about something? Ask directly — a real answer beats a page of small print.

Ask a question
What vaccinations does the puppy have?

By eight weeks the puppy has had two deworming courses and its first complex vaccination, entered in an international vet passport. The second shot falls three weeks later, already in your care — we send a written schedule and remind you when it is due.

Can you deliver abroad?

Yes. Puppies from this kennel live in Germany, Poland, the UK, Canada and the UAE. Flights are booked as cabin baggage with a courier where the airline allows it. Export paperwork — rabies titre test, EU health certificate, TRACES — is prepared by us; the cost of transport is quoted separately and openly.

What is included in the price?

KSU/FCI pedigree documents, vet passport with vaccinations, microchip, a starter pack of the food the puppy is used to, and health results for both parents. Nothing on that list is an upsell.

Can I come and see the puppies?

After the third week, by appointment. Before that the litter is kept quiet for the mother's sake. If you cannot travel to Kyiv we do a live video call instead — no edited clips, just the puppies as they are that day.

How long is the waiting list?

Usually one to two litters ahead, which is three to eight months. A deposit reserves your place in the queue rather than a specific puppy, and it is fully refundable if nothing suits you when the litter arrives.
